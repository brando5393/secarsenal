Minicom is a text-mode serial communications program modelled on the MS-DOS program Telix. It drives modems and serial consoles through a full-screen, menu-driven interface and emulates ANSI and VT102 terminals.

Only use this tool against systems you own or are explicitly authorized to test — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer).
