Autopsy is a graphical digital forensics platform built on top of The
Sleuth Kit, providing case management, timeline analysis, keyword
search, and a modular ingest pipeline for examining disk images and
other evidence.

Only use it on systems/media you own or are explicitly authorized to
examine — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer).
