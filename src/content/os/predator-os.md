Predator-OS is an Iranian-developed, Debian-based distro aiming to
cover penetration testing, privacy, and system hardening all in one
image, at a scale (~1,200 claimed tools) comparable to Kali or
BlackArch. Unlike those two, it doesn't publish a structured,
per-tool source this site can verify or sync from.

Use it only against systems/networks you own or are explicitly
authorized to test — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer).
