Pentoo is a security-focused live CD/USB distribution built on Gentoo
Linux, aimed at users who want a pentesting tool set on top of Gentoo's
source-based, highly configurable package management rather than a
Debian- or Arch-based base.

Use it only against systems you own or are explicitly authorized to
test — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer).
