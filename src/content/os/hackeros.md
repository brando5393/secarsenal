HackerOS is a Polish Debian-based distro whose Cybersecurity Edition is
aimed specifically at red-team work, distinguishing it from the
project's other (gaming, NVIDIA-optimized) editions that aren't
security-focused and aren't covered by this entry.

Use it only against systems/networks you own or are explicitly
authorized to test — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer).
