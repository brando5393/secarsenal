Berserk Arch targets security researchers and developers who want a
fast, current Arch base with security tooling pre-tuned in, rather
than the exhaustive tool-repository approach of BlackArch.

Use it only against systems/networks you own or are explicitly
authorized to test — see the [disclaimer](/disclaimer).
